Quick answer: match days, parks and date flexibility

Choose the visit you can comfortably fit into your trip.

  • One day, one park: a useful starting point if your group mainly wants the castle and classic Disney setting, or mainly wants the stories and attractions in the second park. Choose that park explicitly.
  • One day, two parks: consider this if your short list spans both parks and you accept a selective visit. Moving between parks, meals and queues uses part of the same day.
  • Two or more days: compare multi-day admission if Disney is a major trip priority, you want both parks, or children and other group members need a gentler pace.
  • A fixed visit date: start with dated tickets. A date still undecided: compare undated options only after checking registration availability, expiry and exclusions.
Before checkout: confirm duration, park count, visit dates, guest ages, ticket format, registration requirements, excluded dates and cancellation terms. Check transport and accommodation before paying for a day you may not be able to use.

Disneyland Park vs Disney Adventure World

Two separate theme parks within Disneyland Paris.

Disneyland Park: the castle and classic Disney lands

Disneyland Park has Sleeping Beauty Castle, Main Street, U.S.A., Fantasyland, Adventureland, Frontierland and Discoveryland. Its official park guide lists experiences such as Pirates of the Caribbean, Big Thunder Mountain and Peter Pan's Flight. Start here if the castle setting and these stories are your group's main reason to visit.

Make a short list rather than assuming every attraction will fit into one day. Check restrictions and operations for each priority, and leave space for meals and rest.

Disney Adventure World: Frozen, Pixar and MARVEL stories

Official name checked October 8, 2026: the resort's second theme park is Disney Adventure World, formerly Walt Disney Studios Park. The official Disney Adventure World guide features World of Frozen, Marvel Avengers Campus and Worlds of Pixar, with attractions including Frozen Ever After, Ratatouille: L’Aventure Totalement Toquée de Rémy and Avengers Assemble: Flight Force.

Choose it when these stories and attractions lead your group's list. Check current operating information and any experience-specific booking requirements; choosing this park does not secure a particular ride or character encounter.

Ticket duration, park count and guest categories

These are separate decisions, even when sold together.

The official park pages describe dated admission for one to four days and one-day undated admission. Use the official ticket selector to see the combinations and prices offered for your dates. Multi-day options are presented for exploring both parks; inspect the selected product for covered days, park access and any consecutive-day requirement.

One-day admission: reserve the full outing in your itinerary, including the journey from Paris. Multi-day admission: compare the complete stay cost and the time available for both parks, rather than judging only the ticket total. Two park days with an overnight stay have different transport needs from two return trips from central Paris.

Age categories checked October 8, 2026: official ticket guidance identifies children aged 3–11 and free admission for under-3s. Select the age band for each guest at checkout; ages 12 and above fall outside that child band. The international selector excludes Basic dated tickets from its advertised child rate, so do not assume every product has a separate child price. Ride restrictions and transport fares use their own rules.

  • Dates and duration: confirm the start date and every covered visit day, not just the number of days in the listing title.
  • Park access: verify one park or both, and any restrictions on changing parks.
  • Guests: use ages applicable to the visit, and check eligibility evidence for any special product.
  • Inclusions: confirm whether the purchase is admission only or a package containing transport, a hotel or optional services.
  • Price: compare the checkout total for the same dates, ages and inclusions. Date, duration and product affect the quote; this guide does not publish live prices or discounts.

Dated vs undated: registration, expiry and exclusions

Flexible date choice still requires a usable visit date.

Under the official registration guidance checked October 8, 2026, a ticket showing a specific date does not require separate advance registration. An undated ticket requires registration for your chosen date, subject to availability. A Disney Hotel package that includes park tickets does not require that separate registration; a room booking alone does not establish ticket inclusion.

The official selector advertises one-year validity for undated tickets. Confirm your product's exact start and expiry dates and excluded visit dates before buying. Official information includes exclusions for some holiday dates; do not assume a year's validity makes every date eligible. Check the selected product and registration calendar together.

For an undated purchase, register the correct ticket reference, keep the confirmation with the admission ticket, and check how to amend or cancel the registration if your plans change. A registration cancellation does not itself refund the ticket. For any reseller product, follow the seller's instructions for the ticket number and any required exchange.

After booking: prepare the admission document

  • Check names, visit dates, age bands and park access against the order you intended to place.
  • Read the barcode or QR-code instructions, including whether mobile display or printing is required.
  • If you receive a voucher, follow its exchange instructions; an order receipt is not automatically an admission ticket.
  • Complete required undated-ticket registration and keep the confirmation available.
  • Save documents in the instructed format and keep the seller's contact details available before travel.

Changes and cancellations: inspect the exact product

Do not apply one ticket's policy to every booking.

Official conditions checked October 8, 2026: eligible dated tickets can be cancelled and refunded up to the published three-day deadline. The international ticket conditions explicitly exclude Basic dated tickets. To change an eligible official dated ticket, the published process requires cancelling and buying again, subject to availability and the new price.

Check the precise deadline, time zone and cancellation route in your confirmation. If you booked through Klook or another seller, use that product's conditions and support process; the official direct-purchase procedure may not apply. Hotel packages and optional extras have separate conditions.

An undated ticket's expiry, its registered visit date and its refund conditions are three different things. Do not interpret “flexible” as an unrestricted refund promise. Check the applicable booking terms, including any withdrawal rights for a distance purchase, before deciding which product suits uncertain plans.

Getting there: RER A, rail connections and airports

Marne-la-Vallée–Chessy is the station beside the parks.

From central Paris

Use an RER A train bound for Marne-la-Vallée–Chessy. The line branches, so check the train's destination rather than boarding any eastbound service. The RATP station page provides current station and service information. Plan your hotel-to-platform route, a suitable transport ticket and the return before leaving.

From Gare du Nord, the official travel guide describes RER B to Châtelet–Les Halles, then RER A to Marne-la-Vallée–Chessy. Rail journeys via Lille and paid Paris shuttle options are also described there. Check the actual connection, departure point and inclusions for your booking.

After an airport arrival

Compare the options for your arrival airport, terminal and landing time. Disney's general access guide (French) describes TGV access from Paris-Charles de Gaulle to Marne-la-Vallée–Chessy. The English travel guide also lists Magical Shuttle connections from Charles de Gaulle and Orly. Check rail schedules, shuttle stops, luggage arrangements and accessible-vehicle requirements directly before booking.

Allow time for immigration, bags and the transfer from your terminal. Avoid treating a flight arrival day as a full park day. Transfers, shuttle departures and rail seats are separate from ordinary park admission unless the selected package explicitly includes them.

Use the Paris metro and passes guide, Paris day-trip rail planning and Paris airport transfer guide for the wider trip. Recheck operator notices and your last practical connection home, especially if staying for evening entertainment.

Where to stay: central Paris or near the resort?

Choose the base around the balance of city and park days.

Central Paris: useful when museums, neighborhoods and city evenings dominate your trip. Check the complete RER journey and late return from the hotel you are considering. Our Paris hotel area guide compares city bases; it is not a Disneyland resort-hotel directory.

Near the resort: consider this when you have several park days or want easier rest breaks. Compare walking routes, shuttle schedules, accessibility, breakfast and luggage storage for each property. Do not assume every nearby hotel includes admission or the same shuttle service.

Hotel + Tickets package: read the exact guest coverage and covered dates before buying separate tickets. Compare the package with accommodation and admission bought independently, including transport costs and cancellation terms. Confirm any hotel benefit in the actual booking conditions.

Plan the visit around priorities, breaks and current information

A ticket choice is only the beginning of the day plan.

  • Dates: compare days that fit your wider trip. School holidays, weekends and seasonal events can put pressure on availability; this is a planning consideration, not a prediction of a quiet day.
  • Official information: check the attraction directory and operating notices for your priorities. Use the park guides for current maps and opening information.
  • App setup: install the official Disneyland Paris app and prepare your Disney Account. It supports maps, restaurant bookings and paid extras; some features require location permissions and a connection. Bring a charged phone and a way to recharge it.
  • Families: compare individual height and participation restrictions, plan stroller and baby-care needs, and agree on meeting points. Admission age bands do not determine which attractions a child can use.
  • Accessibility: consult the official accessibility guide for attraction restrictions, maps and access-card eligibility evidence. Ask about your particular requirements before booking; a card does not promise immediate boarding.
  • Weather and meals: pack suitable layers, rain protection and comfortable shoes. Leave time for meals, breaks and a return plan if outdoor activities change. Shows and attractions can be modified or unavailable.

Optional Disney Premier Access

Disney Premier Access is separate from admission. One covers a selected attraction with an allocated time slot; Ultimate covers eligible attractions under its own usage rules. Check current eligibility and availability before buying. Neither replaces park admission, guarantees immediate access, nor ensures an attraction will operate.

Decide whether an optional purchase fits the attractions your group can and wants to experience. Keep a workable day plan even if a paid option or a desired experience is unavailable.

Disneyland Paris ticket FAQs

Park access, registration, booking and arrival.

Should I choose one park or two parks at Disneyland Paris?

Choose one park when its attractions are your main priority and you want a focused day. Choose two parks when your must-see list spans Disneyland Park and Disney Adventure World. A one-day two-park ticket divides the same day between them; extra days can leave more room for breaks.

What is the second Disneyland Paris park called now?

As checked on October 8, 2026, the official name is Disney Adventure World. Older guides may call it Walt Disney Studios Park. It is a separate theme park from Disneyland Park, so check which park or parks your ticket covers.

Do dated and undated tickets need advance registration?

The official registration guidance says a ticket with a specific visit date does not require separate advance registration. An undated ticket requires registration for your chosen date, subject to availability. Check the ticket and seller instructions before travel; an undated ticket alone does not secure any date you choose.

Can I change or cancel my Disneyland Paris ticket?

Conditions depend on the product and seller. Eligible official dated tickets can be cancelled within the published deadline, but Basic dated tickets are excluded from that general policy. Changing an eligible official dated ticket can require cancellation and a new purchase. Cancelling an undated-ticket registration is different from refunding the ticket.

How do I get to Disneyland Paris from central Paris?

Take an RER A service bound for Marne-la-Vallée–Chessy, the station beside the Disney Parks. Check the destination because the line branches. Plan the journey from your hotel and the return with current operator information, including disruptions and the last service suitable for your route.

Does a Disneyland Paris ticket include Disney Premier Access?

Park admission and Disney Premier Access are separate purchases. Premier Access One covers a selected attraction and allocated time slot; Ultimate covers eligible attractions under its own terms. Availability is limited, and these services do not guarantee immediate boarding or that an attraction will operate.

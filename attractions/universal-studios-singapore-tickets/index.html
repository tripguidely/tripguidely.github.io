Is Universal Studios Singapore worth it?

For many travelers, yes. It is especially worth considering if you want a theme park day that is easy to understand, easy to book and easy to fit into Singapore without too much friction.

It tends to make the most sense for families, couples and first-time visitors who want a fun attraction day without needing a full resort-style plan. If you prefer cleaner logistics over the biggest possible park footprint, this is often a very good match.

It is less compelling only if your main goal is to build an entire trip around Universal alone. In that case, a larger destination may suit you better. But for a Singapore itinerary, this page is usually about making the smart, practical booking choice rather than chasing the biggest park name.

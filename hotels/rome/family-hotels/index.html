BEFORE YOU BOOK

Make the room and the journey work together

Enter the whole party

Use every adult, child and age. Check the exact maximum and actual beds; a sofa in a photograph or a suite label is not proof of a sleeping place.

Separate kitchen from room service

Adagio documents kitchens for apartments. Do not generalize that to every room or to the other hotels. Check appliances and meal plans in your category.

Check the daily journey

Adagio is in Balduina; Michelangelo is on the San Pietro side; Quirinale is on Via Nazionale. Compare arrivals, steps and current transport for a stroller.

Before you settle on a stay

Which Rome option has a kitchen?

Adagio Rome Vatican documents equipped kitchens in its apartments. Select the apartment category and confirm its equipment and occupancy; do not assume a generic hotel-room listing includes the same facilities.

Can four people stay in every family hotel room?

No. Quirinale publishes a Junior Suite for up to four, and Adagio lists a one-bedroom apartment for four. Actual beds and the precise offer must still be checked. No four-person Michelangelo room is established here.

Is Adagio beside the Vatican entrance?

Its address is Via Damiano Chiesa 8 in Balduina. Plan the actual journey to the Vatican rather than treating the property name as a promise of immediate walking access.

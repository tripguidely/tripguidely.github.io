Before you settle on a stay

Which shortlist options are hostels?

The Beehive and Generator Rome describe hostel accommodation, including private and shared formats. Hotel Sonya is the hotel-room alternative. Check whether the booking covers a private room or one dorm bed.

Is there a guaranteed cheapest Rome hotel?

No. Dates, guest counts, room formats, extras and cancellation terms change the comparison. These are selected options, not a verified lowest-price ranking.

What needs special attention at The Beehive?

Check its published arrival reception hours and group-size rule. Compare the exact bathroom and air-conditioning arrangements for the selected room, and confirm any late arrival.

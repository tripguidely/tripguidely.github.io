Before you settle on a stay

Which of these luxury hotels is near the Spanish Steps?

Hotel de Russie is on Via del Babuino, Hotel Manfredi Suite in Rome is on Via Margutta, and Hotel Eden is on Via Ludovisi. Use the exact address and the route you will walk, rather than a neighborhood name alone.

Is Hotel Manfredi the Colosseum property?

This shortlist uses Hotel Manfredi Suite in Rome at Via Margutta 61. It is distinct from Palazzo Manfredi by the Colosseum. Check that identity and address when reviewing the offer.

Does a luxury booking guarantee a suite or view?

No. The exact room category and written inclusions determine the layout, outlook and services. A hotel name or photograph is not a guarantee.

One bed or two bunks?

The official Single Pod sleeps one guest in a twin bed. Bunk, Queen and Full Pods list two guests; a Full Pod is recommended by the hotel for solo travel. These categories list private bathrooms. A bunk room provides twin beds arranged vertically, rather than two side-by-side beds.

THE USEFUL QUESTIONS

Before you settle on a stay

Is citizenM always a cheap hotel?

No. It is included as a compact-room value alternative, not as a guaranteed budget rate. Compare its total for your exact dates against equivalent offers at the two Pod properties.

Do Pod 39 and Pod Times Square have the same address?

No. Pod 39 is at 145 East 39th Street in Murray Hill. Pod Times Square is at 400 West 42nd Street. Keep the property, room category and booking link matched.

Can I book separate beds at citizenM Times Square?

The current official description presents rooms for two adults with an XL king bed. Do not select this option on the assumption that it supplies twin beds; verify the actual offer before booking.

What should I compare beyond the displayed nightly rate?

Use the final total for the same dates and guests, then compare mandatory charges, included services, cancellation terms and the room configuration. No fixed nightly price or availability is claimed here.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

Spend on the part of the stay you will actually use

A premium address, a named view and a particular room layout are different purchases. Decide whether the stay centers on Central Park, Midtown sightseeing or Downtown plans before comparing rates. Then check that the advertised room category includes the feature you care about.

01

Choose your part of Manhattan

Two of these options are in Midtown; Conrad is in Lower Manhattan. Build the shortlist around the places you intend to spend time.

02

Read the category, not just the name

A terrace, kitchen, club access or skyline view belongs to a specific room or offer. Do not assume it appears in every category.

03

Compare the complete booking

Check the final total, inclusions, deposit, payment timing and cancellation conditions. Treat dining and other extras as separate unless explicitly included.

Keep this checklist: Exact room name; stated view; included services; cancellation deadline; final payable total.

THE USEFUL QUESTIONS

Before you settle on a stay

Which of these luxury hotels is Downtown?

Conrad New York Downtown is at 102 North End Avenue in Battery Park City. The Plaza and The Langham are on Fifth Avenue in Midtown. Choose between those locations before comparing room categories.

Does The Plaza guarantee a Central Park view?

No blanket view guarantee follows from the property name. Book a category that explicitly describes the view you want, and check the details of that particular offer.

Does every Langham room include a kitchen?

No. The official room list names selected kitchen categories separately. Match the exact category on the booking page to the facilities you need.

Are breakfast and special hotel experiences included?

Only when your selected offer says so. Check breakfast, dining reservations, club access and other inclusions separately rather than assuming a luxury rate covers them.

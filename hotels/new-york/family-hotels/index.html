THE USEFUL QUESTIONS

Before you settle on a stay

Can I assume every suite sleeps my whole family?

No. Accepted occupancy, age rules and bed layouts belong to the selected room. Enter all guests and verify the written configuration before paying. Ask the provider to clarify anything that is not explicit.

Are the two Residence Inn hotels the same property?

No. The Central Park property is at 1717 Broadway. The Times Square property is at 1033 Avenue of the Americas, near Bryant Park. Check both the name and address when following a booking link.

Do all three hotels offer kitchen facilities?

Their official property descriptions list kitchens or kitchenette facilities. The equipment and layout can differ. Confirm what the selected category includes instead of treating kitchen and kitchenette as identical.

Are connecting rooms or cribs guaranteed?

Do not treat a listed service or a request as a confirmed arrangement. Verify age rules, charges if applicable and written confirmation of the exact room combination or crib before relying on it.

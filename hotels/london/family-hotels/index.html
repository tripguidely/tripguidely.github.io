Before you settle on a stay

Which London options have kitchens?

Staybridge describes equipped suite kitchens, Marriott describes kitchens at Residence Inn Kensington, and Citadines lists apartment kitchenettes. Confirm the named category and equipment in the selected Klook offer.

Can four people stay in every apartment?

No. Citadines lists its studio for up to two, one-bedroom apartment for up to four and two-bedroom apartment for up to six. The actual beds and accepted party must still match the booked category. No blanket four-person capacity is claimed for the other hotels.

Does a sofa always mean a sofa bed?

No. Some Residence Inn suite descriptions name sofa beds, but the exact category matters. Request written confirmation of every sleeping place rather than inferring it from a photograph.

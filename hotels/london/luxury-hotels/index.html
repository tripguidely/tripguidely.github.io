BEFORE YOU BOOK

Make the room and the journey work together

Put the address before the star rating

For a five-star hotel in London, compare the actual neighborhood as carefully as the room. The Savoy suits Strand and Covent Garden plans; The Shard is at London Bridge; The Langham is by Oxford Circus.

Read the view category

River, courtyard and city-facing rooms are different offers. Check the named outlook rather than using a facade or pool photograph as a promise.

Separate the room from the experience

Verify breakfast, club access, spa or pool conditions and dining reservations. Compare deposits, cancellation timing and the full payable total.

Compare the full payable total and any separate mandatory charges. Photographs and published categories do not establish availability or inclusions for your dates.

Before you settle on a stay

Which option is near Covent Garden?

The Savoy is on the Strand, on the Covent Garden side of the West End. The Langham is by Oxford Circus, while Shangri-La The Shard is at London Bridge south of the Thames.

Does The Savoy guarantee a Thames view?

No. Its published room list distinguishes city, courtyard, garden and river-view categories. Select the room that explicitly states the outlook you want.

Does every Shangri-La room face the same landmarks?

No. Published room categories describe different directions and city outlooks. Match the named view to your priorities rather than assuming every window gives the same panorama.
